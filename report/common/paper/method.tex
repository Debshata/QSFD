\section{Experimental Methodology}\label{sec:method}
\paragraph{Environment.} Experiments ran on Windows~11 with Python~3.14, Qiskit~2.5.2, Qiskit~Aer~0.17.2 and \texttt{pqcrypto}~\PqcryptoVersion{} (full metadata in \path{results/layer2/pqc_benchmark_meta.json} and \path{results/final/final_results.json}).

\paragraph{PQC benchmarking.} Timing used \path{time.perf_counter_ns} with warm-up runs; import and file-writing time were excluded. Compiled operations used 200 repetitions (30 for SLH-DSA-128s key generation and signing); reference operations used 30 repetitions, except reference SLH-DSA key generation and signing (3) and verification (10), because a single reference SLH-DSA signature takes seconds. Repetition counts are therefore unequal and always reported. Statistics: mean, median, standard deviation, minimum, maximum, P5, P95 and a $t$-based 95\% interval.

\paragraph{End-to-end pipeline.} A canonical JSON transaction is hashed, signed, protected with a derived session key and AES-256-GCM, passed in memory, decrypted and verified. Configurations: classical (A); reference ML-KEM-768 + ML-DSA-65 (B); compiled ML-KEM-768 + ML-DSA-65 (C); hybrid ECDH + ML-KEM with ECDSA + ML-DSA (D); and SLH-DSA-128s variants of B and C.

\paragraph{BB84.} Protocol simulator: 100 trials per condition at 500 and at 2000 transmitted bits (seeds 20000+trial). Qiskit/Aer: 50 trials per condition at 500 bits (seeds 30000+trial). Conditions: clean, 1\%, 5\% and 10\% noise, intercept--resend, and intercept--resend with 2\% noise. QBER is computed over the full sifted key. Agreement is summarized by mean absolute error, root-mean-square error and the maximum absolute difference of mean QBER, not by correlation. Detection thresholds from 0 to 0.40 are swept, treating Eve-absent trials as negatives (two noise ranges) and Eve-present trials as positives.

\paragraph{IBM Quantum hardware.} A pilot job on \HwBackend{} (17 circuits, 1024 shots) preceded a replicated campaign of \HwJobs{} independent jobs with \HwShots{} shots per circuit. The qubit-selection rule was fixed from the calibration snapshot before any job: score $=$ readout error $+$ single-qubit gate error among qubits with reported T1, T2, readout and gate errors and readout error $\le 0.2$; the \emph{best} qubit has the lowest score, the \emph{typical} qubit the median rank and the \emph{worse} qubit the 90th percentile. Each job contained matched, mismatched and intercept--resend-model BB84 circuits on each qubit and a two-qubit Grover circuit at optimization levels 0--3 on the best-calibrated coupled pair. Ideal and backend-derived noisy Aer controls used the same mappings and transpiler seed with 8192 shots; the noisy model used backend properties at analysis time. All raw counts, job IDs and calibration snapshots are stored under \path{results/ibm_quantum/}; analysis runs on saved files only. Statistics: mean, standard deviation and $t$-interval over jobs, paired bootstrap and exact sign-flip permutation across jobs (with $n=5$ the smallest attainable one-sided $p$ is $1/32$, so effect sizes and intervals are primary).

\paragraph{Reproducibility.} Authoritative values live in \path{results/final/final_results.json}, generated from the saved artifacts; all LaTeX numbers here are macros generated from it. The test suite has \TestsTotal{} tests (\TestsPassed{} passed, \TestsFailed{} failed, \TestsSkipped{} skipped). Commands: \path{python scripts/security/run_pqc_backend_benchmarks.py}, \path{run_end_to_end_banking.py}, \path{run_bb84_final.py}, \path{python scripts/ibm_quantum/analyze_campaign.py A}, \path{python scripts/final/build_final_results.py}.
